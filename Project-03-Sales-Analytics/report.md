# Sales Analytics Dashboard Report

## Objective

Analyze 30 days of sales data to identify business trends, revenue patterns, and sales performance using NumPy and Matplotlib.

---

## Dataset

- Total Days: 30
- Metric: Daily Sales

---

## Analysis

### Revenue

The business generated consistent daily revenue with an upward sales trend throughout the month.

### Daily Sales

Sales increased steadily over the 30-day period, indicating positive business growth.

### Distribution

Most daily sales fell within the middle range, with a gradual increase toward the end of the month.

### Moving Average

The 3-day moving average smoothed daily fluctuations and clearly highlighted the upward trend.

### Business Performance

More days recorded above-average sales toward the end of the month, reflecting improving performance.

---

## Conclusion

NumPy was used to calculate key business metrics, while Matplotlib provided clear visualizations that support business decision-making.
