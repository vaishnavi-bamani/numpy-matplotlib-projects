# Weather Dashboard Report

## Objective

Analyze one month of weather data using NumPy and visualize insights using Matplotlib.

---

## Dataset

- Days: 30
- Features:
  - Temperature
  - Rainfall
  - Humidity

---

## Analysis

### Temperature

- Temperature remained between 29°C and 36°C.
- The hottest period occurred around Day 20.
- Overall temperature remained fairly stable.

### Rainfall

- Rainfall occurred on several days.
- Some days recorded heavy rainfall above 15 mm.
- Many days had no rainfall.

### Humidity

- Humidity mostly ranged between 55% and 70%.
- Very humid days corresponded with rainy periods.

### Weather Summary

- The month included both rainy and dry days.
- Dry days slightly outnumbered rainy days.

---

## Conclusion

NumPy was used for numerical analysis, while Matplotlib helped transform the raw data into meaningful visual insights through multiple chart types.
